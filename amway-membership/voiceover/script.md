# Voiceover script (optional)

Record it in VoiceStudio on your Mac, export one WAV per scene (or one long WAV),
and send it back. I'll mix it under the music. Times are where each scene starts
in the current cut (3:24). Each line is written to fit its scene at a calm pace
(about 2.5 words a second). If a take runs long, lengthen that scene's `dur` in
`src/content.js`.

Accuracy rules carried over: always "up to" on $100 / 5× / $300 / $1,200. Never say
"use it once a year and it pays for the whole year."
